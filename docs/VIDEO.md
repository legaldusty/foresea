# Foresea demo video, 2 minutes

One take, one screen recording, voice over the top. Read this once all the way through, do the setup, practice it one time, then record.

## Setup before you hit record (about 5 minutes)

1. Turn on Do Not Disturb on the Mac so nothing pops up. Close Slack, Messages and every other app.
2. Open Chrome and go to https://foresea.imdusty.com. When the welcome box shows up, click **Explore on my own**.
3. Click **Views** in the top right, then the small arrow next to **Captain phone** to open it in a new tab. Drag that tab out so it becomes its own window.
4. Make the captain window narrow like a phone (about 420 pixels wide, as tall as the screen) and put it on the right edge of the screen. Put the desk window on the left and make it fill the rest of the screen.
5. In the captain window, click **Join**. When the little tour pops up, click the **X** to close it.
6. In the desk window, press the **P** key once to open the Presenter panel, then press **P** again to close it. You just want to know where it is. Make sure the desk says **Live** at the top left.
7. Practice the whole thing once without recording. When you are done practicing, click **Live** at the top left of the desk to reset the harbor for the real take. The phone window stays connected because both windows share the harbor code in the address bar.
8. Start the recording: press **Cmd + Shift + 5**, pick **Record Entire Screen**, set Options > Microphone to your Mac mic, and click **Record**. Or record with no sound and add your voice later in iMovie or CapCut.

Talk slow and clear. If something is a little late, keep talking. You can trim dead time when you edit.

## The shot list

### 0:00 to 0:12, the hook (desk, Live mode)

**Do:** Let the live map sit for a second, then click one of the gray ships on the chart so its card opens on the right.

**Say:** "This is the Port of LA, live. Every ship here is real. When something unknown shows up underwater, ports shut down, and that can cost 150 million dollars a day."

### 0:12 to 0:20, start the night

**Do:** Press **P**. In the Presenter panel click **Night**, then **20x**, then **A, The straight shot**. Press **P** again to hide the panel.

**Say:** "Foresea is our fix. Let's play a night where an unknown drone sneaks in at Angels Gate."

### 0:20 to 0:40, the forecast

**Do:** Do not click anything. Move your mouse slowly along the red area on the map as it narrows toward the Fuel Pier. The sentence at the top will change to say it is heading to the Fuel Pier.

**Say:** "First it figures out what this thing is from its size, heat and speed. Then our model, TELL, predicts where it's going. Yellow is everywhere it could be. Red is where it's most likely headed. Watch it lock onto the Fuel Pier as it learns from every move."

### 0:40 to 0:52, the gentlest fix

**Do:** On the right, the card says **Publish this notice**. Run your mouse down the boat rows and the Total line.

**Say:** "Instead of closing the port, it drafts the gentlest fix. Only these few boats change course, about two minutes of delay, and nothing closes."

If the card shows **Publishing on your behalf in 20 s**, click **Stop** first, so you can show that a person is in charge.

### 0:52 to 1:06, one tap reaches the phone

**Do:** Click **Publish** on the card. The captain window on the right turns red with **P1 Act now** and a big instruction. Click **Got it** in the captain window. On the desk, the Boats told list shows Harbor Launch 7 as acknowledged.

**Say:** "A person always approves. One tap, and the captain in the path gets one clear instruction, a countdown and a new route. Boats out of the way don't get bothered."

### 1:06 to 1:14, someone ignores it

**Do:** Wait for the card **Pilot Boat 2 not responding** with a radio script. Point at it with the mouse. (If it has not shown up yet, skip this part and keep going.)

**Say:** "If a captain ignores it, the officer gets a radio script to call them right away."

### 1:14 to 1:30, the dual use part

**Do:** Press **P**, click **Tuesday**, press **P** again. The bottom of the desk shows **Today** with the dive, the dredge and the whale zones. Click the **Blue whale** row so the map jumps to it.

**Say:** "Now the dual use part. The same engine runs the harbor on a normal day. Dive teams, a dredge, a whale slow zone and real ship arrival times, all on one screen."

### 1:30 to 1:46, person overboard

**Do:** In the captain window, click the red **SOS** button. Press and hold **Hold to send** for two full seconds and let go. The desk gets a card that says **Person overboard from Harbor Launch 7**. Click **Publish search area** on the desk.

**Say:** "And if someone falls overboard, the captain just holds SOS. The same math that tracks drones becomes a search area that drifts with the real ocean current."

### 1:46 to 2:00, proof and the ending

**Do:** Press **P** and click **Run 1,000 nights**. Let the numbers fill in. End on that screen.

**Say:** "In a thousand simulated nights, Foresea gave 74 minutes of warning, way more than just guessing. Foresea. See it coming, so the port never has to stop."

Stop the recording.

## Editing (10 minutes)

1. Open the video in iMovie or CapCut.
2. Cut anything before the hook and after the ending.
3. If you waited too long anywhere, speed that part up or trim it so the whole video is under 2:00.
4. Add one title card at the very end, 2 seconds long, that says **foresea.imdusty.com**.
5. Export at 1080p and upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**. Paste the link into Devpost.

## If something goes wrong while recording

If the red never locks onto the Fuel Pier, the drone may have picked another dock. That is fine. Say "watch it lock onto its target" and keep going.

If the captain window does not turn red after Publish, it means Harbor Launch 7 was not in the path this time. Press **P**, click **Night**, then **A** again, and redo from 0:12. Publish a little sooner, while the red is still short.

If anything looks stuck, refresh the desk window. Your private harbor and the phone window stay connected.
