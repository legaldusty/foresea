## Twist 3: make a poster

The prompt: visualization is a big part of how we communicate ideas, so make a poster for the solution, 8.5 by 11, and submit it with Devpost by 10 PM. Strong posters get printed for demo day on Sunday. The size was later changed to 3 by 4 feet, so the same design was scaled up as one piece in Figma.

What we made: a one page poster designed in Figma (page Poster, frame `115:33`), built only from our brand and our real product. The Foresea lockup and colors, IBM Plex type, a headline that says the whole idea ("See it coming. Keep the port open."), real screenshots of the desk forecast, the captain's P1 alert and the person overboard screen, the four steps (identify, predict, give way, a person decides), the dual use list, the proof numbers from our benchmark (74 minutes of warning, 97% inside the forecast, 0 gates closed) and a QR code to the live site that we decoded to check it opens https://foresea.imdusty.com.

Files: `docs/devpost/Foresea Poster 36x48.pdf` (one page, 36 by 48 in, for printing) and `docs/devpost/Foresea Poster 36x48.png` (5400 by 7200, 150 dpi). The screenshot strip was rebuilt from the full resolution captures so it prints sharp at that size. The printed version leaves out the bottom band with the proof numbers and the QR code, and the two sections above it grow to fill the space. The letter size versions, `Foresea Poster.pdf` and `Foresea Poster.png`, are kept for handouts.
